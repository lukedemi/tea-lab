# House brewing method

Every cup, unless a recipe says otherwise:

- **Water:** 208°F
- **Mug:** pre-warmed (fill with hot water, dump before brewing)
- **Infuser:** Finum reusable stainless steel mesh brewing basket, lid on while steeping
- **Volume:** 11 oz (325 ml), the usual mug

## Steep times at 208°F
| Blend type | Time |
|---|---|
| Leafy (mint) | 6-8 min |
| Fruit and flower (hibiscus, rosehips) | 6-8 min |
| Root-heavy (ginger, licorice, elderberry) | 10 min, roots chopped small |

## Notes
- Keep the Finum lid on: it holds in mint and spice aroma and keeps the temperature up.
- Roots extract slowly without a simmer. Chop them to 3-5 mm and give them the longer steep.
- Rosehip hairs are fine. If the cup feels scratchy, pour it through a paper coffee filter too.
- Lift the basket out at the end of the steep, so the cup doesn't keep getting stronger and more bitter.

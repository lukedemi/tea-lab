# Golden Ember, v1

Original blend. Warm, spicy and gently sweet. No fruit or flowers, so it's the opposite of Ruby Ginger.

| Ingredient | Grams | % |
|---|---|---|
| Ginger root, chopped small | 3.20 | 64 |
| Cinnamon (broken stick) | 1.20 | 24 |
| Licorice root | 0.45 | 9 |
| Ground turmeric | 0.15 | 3 |
| **Total** | **5.00** | |

Brew (house method, see [BREWING.md](../BREWING.md)): 208°F, 11 oz, Finum basket with lid on. Steep 10 min (root-heavy). Some turmeric powder passes the mesh: stir before drinking, and leave the last sip.

Cost: about $0.37 per cup (store prices; cinnamon estimated from Mountain Rose Ceylon sticks; turmeric about a cent).

# Elderberry Healer (Rishi copy), v1

Based on Rishi's ingredient order: ginger > elderberries > hibiscus > licorice.

| Ingredient | Grams | % |
|---|---|---|
| Ginger root | 4.21 | 42.1 |
| Elderberries | 3.16 | 31.6 |
| Hibiscus flowers | 2.11 | 21.1 |
| Licorice root | 0.52 | 5.2 |
| **Total** | **10.00** | |

Brew: 16 oz (475 ml) water. Bring to a boil with the blend and simmer 5 min, then steep 5 min off the heat. Strain.

## Tasting notes

- v1 visual vs Rishi photo: my ginger pieces are much bigger (1-2 cm vs Rishi's ~3-5 mm bits), so ginger looks dominant; Rishi's pile reads mostly hibiscus. Hibiscus is light and fluffy, so it fills more space per gram. Try chopping ginger smaller next time.

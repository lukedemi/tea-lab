# Everyday Mint

Spearmint-forward daily cup. No licorice: sweetened with Splenda in the mug.

## A: Warm (v1)
| Ingredient | Grams | % |
|---|---|---|
| Spearmint | 3.20 | 80 |
| Ginger root, chopped small | 0.60 | 15 |
| Cinnamon (broken stick) | 0.20 | 5 |
| **Total** | **4.00** | |

## B: Bright (v1)
| Ingredient | Grams | % |
|---|---|---|
| Spearmint | 3.40 | 85 |
| Hibiscus flowers | 0.35 | 8.75 |
| Rosehips, crushed to pea-size | 0.25 | 6.25 |
| **Total** | **4.00** | |

Brew (house method, see [BREWING.md](../BREWING.md)): 208°F, 11 oz mug, Finum basket with lid on. Steep 7 min. Sweeten with Splenda after.

## Batch (A, 200 g, about 50 cups)
Spearmint 160 g, ginger 30 g (chopped small), cinnamon 10 g (broken small). Shake the jar before scooping 4 g: small pieces settle.
